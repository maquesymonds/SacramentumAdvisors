Uruguay has quietly built one of Latin America's most export-driven technology
ecosystems.

In a country of just 3.5 million people, the ecosystem brings together around
500 startups, hundreds of established software and technology companies,
global technology centers, internationally successful product companies, and a
NASDAQ-listed unicorn.

Uruguay is Latin America's second-largest software exporter per capita — and
the largest in South America. The technology sector generated US$3.68 billion
in revenue in 2024, equivalent to 4.5% of GDP, with 63% of its revenue coming
from international sales.

The United States remains its strongest market, accounting for 77.6% of
technology exports from Uruguay. At the same time, established links with the
United Kingdom, Spain, Chile, Canada, and other international markets position
Uruguay as a natural technology platform connecting North America, Latin
America, and Europe.

Uruguay combines institutional stability, sophisticated talent, strong digital
