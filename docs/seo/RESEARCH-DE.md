**First conclusions**
1. **German has its own agenda:** the exit tax (*Wegzugsbesteuerung*) and *erweitert beschränkte Steuerpflicht* are the entry door —
   no other language has this. A German page/article on "Auswandern nach Uruguay: Steuern" is the strongest German opportunity.
   Sacramentum is not a German tax advisor: this needs a German *Steuerberater* partner or careful sourcing.
2. **Paraguay owns the German emigration conversation** (German settlements, "Deutsche in Paraguay"). Uruguay must be positioned against it.
3. **Real estate in German = "Haus kaufen in Uruguay"**, with Piriápolis and Punta del Este.
4. **Farmland does not work in German** — keep it in English for German speakers.
5. **Investment vocabulary ("in Uruguay investieren") has no volume** — same as Spanish and English.

**Readings**

1. **Language is a competitive advantage in German.** Gemini goes out of its way to name *German-speaking* advisors (Comans, Reluy,
   Team Haverkate, the embassy's list of German-speaking lawyers). If anyone at Sacramentum speaks German, it must say so prominently;
   if not, the German site should say "we work in English and Spanish, with German-speaking partners".
2. **Uruguayan real estate agents already publish in German** (Santiago Pereyra, Nicolás de Modena, Luxury Punta, Engel & Völkers) and
   win the German AI answer. Same pattern as English: specific, dated articles.
3. **The German entry door is the tax exit — confirmed.** Gemini's answer 1 is a detailed German tax answer (§ 6 AStG: ≥ 1 % share,
   7 of last 12 years; since 2022 no interest-free deferral for non-EU moves, 7-year instalments with security; § 2 AStG: 10 years of
   *erweitert beschränkte Steuerpflicht* for moves to low-tax countries, thresholds €62.000 income / €154.000 assets in Germany).
   All sources are German tax firms. → Sacramentum needs a **German *Steuerberater* partner** to co-author this, not to write it alone.
4. **Double-tax treaty: Gemini says there is none** ("historisches Abkommen gekündigt") — but the source it cites is titled
   *"Seit 2011 DBA zwischen Deutschland und Uruguay"*. The claim is very likely wrong. **Must be verified** before any German tax content.
5. **The 2026 regime appears again, again inconsistently:** answer 2 says 12.5M UI **+ 60 days**; answer 6 says capital gains stay
   tax-free; answer 5 says 12 % on foreign capital income "since 2026". Same gap as ES and EN.
6. **Families:** Deutsche Schule Montevideo (founded 1857, Pocitos and Carrasco, Kindergarten to Abitur, apply ~1 year ahead),
   private schools USD 400–1.300/month, Carrasco / Punta Gorda / La Tahona. A German family page practically writes itself.
7. **Wording to avoid:** "Steuerparadies", "Bankgeheimnis" (Gemini 6 still claims strong banking secrecy — outdated and a compliance
   risk). Use *Rechtssicherheit, Stabilität, Vermögensdiversifikation*.
